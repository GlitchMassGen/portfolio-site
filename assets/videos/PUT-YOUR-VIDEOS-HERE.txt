Put your videos in this folder, then write their file names in js/config.js.

Tips
- Use .mp4 (H.264 video + AAC audio). It plays on every phone and browser.
- iPhone .mov files often will not play in Chrome. Export or convert to .mp4
  (HandBrake, CapCut, or any "convert to mp4" tool).
- Keep each video under about 50 MB. Shorter clips load faster.
- Vertical phone videos are fine; the frame adjusts to the video's shape.
- Optional: a poster image (cover picture) goes in assets/images/ and is
  named with  poster: "assets/images/your-cover.jpg"  in config.js.
