Put your photos in this folder, then write their file names in js/config.js.

Tips
- JPG, PNG or WebP work everywhere. iPhone HEIC files do NOT show in most
  browsers, so export or convert them to JPG first.
- Resize big photos to about 1600 pixels on the longest side (under 1 MB each).
  The page loads much faster, especially on her phone.
- File names with no spaces are safest, e.g. beach-day.jpg
